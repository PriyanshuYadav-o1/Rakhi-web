A LETTER FOR KAVYA

Dear Kavya,

there is so much life waiting for you. You will discover new things, make mistakes, learn from them, meet people, chase dreams and slowly create a world that is completely your own.

I don't want to decide who you should become. I just want you to remember that you never have to handle everything alone. If you need someone to listen, laugh with you, celebrate you or simply stand beside you, your brother is here.

Keep your kindness. Keep your smile. Keep your confidence. And never think you have to change who you are to be amazing.
